Candidate placement office

Manage a candidate across approved US locations, from an accurate profile through applications, interviews, offers, and a confirmed start. Twelve staff roles own twelve linked handoffs. Placement is not guaranteed; these are manual coordination workflows.

New-job recruiting campaign

Monitor up to five Greenhouse employer boards every 15 minutes while this server runs. The first scan establishes a baseline; only previously unseen openings enter the queue afterward. This means newly observed, not a verified publication date. Interviews do not stop discovery. Accepted, started, paused, or withdrawn candidate status stops it.

New-job queue

Drafts use exact CV excerpts. Keyword screening does not confirm full eligibility. Queue entries are never marked submitted without a real submission. Existing baseline openings are excluded.

Job Application Operations

Create a workflow for one opening. Eight teams coordinate nine tasks with prerequisite checks. Portal discovery, CV writing, and submission are manual handoffs until integrations are connected.

Rejection review and improvement

A rejection does not prove a CV mistake. Record employer feedback separately from hypotheses; choose Unknown when no reason was given. A named owner records the corrective check for future applications. These are reviewed records, not automatic learning or a placement guarantee.

Next-application corrective check

Before starting the next submission, review all recorded lessons for that candidate against this opening, the exact CV, and application answers. Explain fixes or why a lesson does not apply. New lessons invalidate previous checks.

Duplicate protection

Register each application before submission. Use the same candidate case, canonical employer domain, and employer requisition ID across all portals. Repeated registrations return the original application; a second project for the same identity is blocked. Existing unregistered workflows require registration before submission.

Application and placement status desk

The Submission Team records what the portal or email actually says. For candidate cases, Started means work actually began, supported by employer, role, start date, and candidate confirmation in the evidence. Saving a record does not apply, contact an employer, or check a portal automatically.
